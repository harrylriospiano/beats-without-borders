Meet Harry

From New Orleans roots and Puerto Rican heritage to stages around the world.

I'm Harry Rios. Music has taken me around the world as a professional pianist and vocalist — but the story behind that music started much closer to home.

Growing up around the sounds of New Orleans — a city built on Creole roots reaching back to France — while carrying my own Puerto Rican heritage taught me that music doesn't belong to just one place or one people. It travels. It mixes. And sometimes the things that make us different become the very things that make what we create extraordinary.

That's the idea behind Beats Without Borders.

A Message for Educators

Hear It From Harry

“My musical story really begins between two cultures — New Orleans and my Puerto Rican heritage. Beats Without Borders is the school program I've created to show students how Africa, Spain, France and the Caribbean came together in New Orleans to help create the music we know today. And underneath the music is an even bigger message: the things that make you different may be exactly where your greatest strengths are hiding.” — Harry Rios

The Experience

You Won't Just Watch.

This isn't a sit-still-and-listen assembly. It's loud, it's live, and every student in the room becomes part of the show.

01

You'll Hear It.

02

03

The room claps back, sings along and gets loud — turning music history into something happening right now, not a lesson about the past.

04

You'll Take Something With You.

Harry connects the musical journey to identity, talent, rejection, confidence and perseverance.

The Musical Journey

Africa → Spain → Caribbean & Puerto Rico → New Orleans → American Music

Beats Without Borders explores some of the most important musical and cultural threads in American music — how rhythms, instruments and traditions traveled, mixed and evolved as they crossed oceans and generations. Through live performance, rhythm and storytelling, students hear these connections instead of just reading about them.
